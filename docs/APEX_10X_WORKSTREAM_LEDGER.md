# APEX-10X Workstream Continuity Ledger

Canonical tracking document for all active, incomplete, recently completed, planned, and strategically relevant workstreams across VA Freelance Hub.

In accordance with Section 6 of the APEX-10X Master Mandate:
- **ACTIVE**: Currently in progress or executing in the active sprint/wave.
- **COMPLEMENT**: Standalone or complementary system supporting the core engine (e.g. analytics, directory pulses).
- **DONE_VERIFIED**: Fully implemented, tested, verified by CI/deploy evidence, and operating stably.
- **BLOCKED**: Held pending a verifiable precondition or observation window (e.g. shadow duration).
- **PAUSED**: Deliberately idled or bounded to prevent resource depletion or compliance exposure.
- **SUPERSEDED_WITH_EVIDENCE**: Replaced by a superior architectural pattern, with exact historical evidence cited.
