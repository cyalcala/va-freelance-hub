## Disposition vocabulary

- **Adopted for fusion**: preserve the idea in the revised prompts, usually by referring to canonical governance rather than duplicating it. This means editorial adoption, not an operational amendment.
- **Needs verification**: preserve as a claim, parameter, optional experiment, or unresolved discrepancy; do not present it as measured reality.
- **Rejected as instruction**: do not execute a direction merely because it appears inside an attached note. Useful underlying content can still be adopted for future authorized work.
- **Duplicate**: consolidate repeated material into one clear contract, retaining provenance here.

## Mathematical and metric corrections for the improved prompts

These are analytical review recommendations, not silent edits to accepted thresholds.

| ID | Issue and cited source | Required interpretation or correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M01 | Core 102–105 subtracts backlog, reactivation and replay from first publications, while G902 1363–1395 explicitly warns against overlapping subtraction. | Define a set of unique canonical first-publication events within the day and assign exactly one primary creation cohort under a documented precedence rule. Count the eligible fresh cohort directly. If set subtraction is used, subtract the **union** of excluded event sets, not a sum of overlapping counts. Reactivations are not first-publication events. Example: one event labeled both backlog and replay must be excluded once, not twice. |
| M02 | G902 1487–1491, 2322–2329; core 118, 228–240. “False-positive rate” and “false PH eligibility rate” do not specify a denominator. | Report separately `FP/(FP+TN)` for false-positive rate and `FP/(TP+FP)` for false discovery among published/accepted items, with independent labels and sample design. A published-item audit alone cannot estimate conventional FPR or recall without rejected/ineligible sampling. Specify whether results are sample estimates or complete-cohort counts. |
| M03 | G902 1676–1694 productivity formula multiplies eligibility, non-duplicate, non-expired, safety, reliability and marginal uniqueness, then divides by cost. | The product is a hypothesis. Multiplying unconditional rates assumes relationships that may not hold and can double-count uniqueness/reliability losses. Prefer an observed deduplicated useful yield per explicitly defined resource unit, or a chain of **conditional** stage-pass probabilities with common cohorts. Define rate units, time window, currency/compute conversion, zero cost treatment and uncertainty before ranking. |
| M04 | G902 2389–2407 AI value-of-information formula credits correct changes but omits harmful changes. | Evaluate expected loss reduction relative to the deterministic baseline: `E[loss_baseline - loss_after_model] - incremental_model_cost - latency_or_operational_cost`, in compatible units. This includes wrong changes and abstentions. An efficiency ratio does not replace positive net expected benefit or safety constraints. |
| M05 | G902 1545–1591 Pareto and lexicographic policy ordering. | Define comparable objectives, direction, feasibility, practical-equivalence tolerances and uncertainty. Point estimates cannot prove dominance when intervals overlap materially. “Optimal” additionally needs a stated feasible action set and evidence that the optimizer solved the declared problem. |
| M06 | G902 1757–1822 diversity, HHI, entropy and removal simulations. | State denominator/cohort and source-attribution convention. HHI and entropy summarize shares, not independence; correlated providers require an evidenced dependency model. Static removal loss estimates supply under a fixed policy, not the adaptive system's complete counterfactual recovery. Do not rename nominal effective source count as “independent” without proving dependency assumptions. |
| M07 | G902 1859–1917 regret and off-policy evaluation. | Rewards for unchosen actions are generally unobserved. Label regret as estimated or policy-relative unless the comparator is known. Log action availability, propensities, context, version and matured outcomes; verify overlap/support. A deterministic logging policy supplies no evidence for never-selected actions. IPS/DR names do not cure missing support, confounding or delayed-label bias. |
| M08 | G902 2026–2082 queue recurrence and Little's law. | Use conservation over a named boundary: queue change equals admitted arrivals minus departures, with expiry, rejection, cancellation and retries accounted for explicitly. `max(0, …)` must not conceal inconsistent counts. `L=λW` refers to matching long-run averages/cohorts under suitable stability and finite-mean conditions; empirical residence-time distributions remain necessary. Bursts alone do not invalidate the identity, and the identity does not prove stability or latency percentiles. |
| M09 | G902 2232–2282 survival and reservoir. | Define age origin, useful/expired event, observation cadence, censoring, competing events and cohort comparability. Kaplan–Meier or exponential curves require assumptions and enough evidence. A 30-day freshness ceiling does not prove a 29-day-old job remains open. Missing posting dates remain unknown under accepted policy. |
| M10 | G902 2940–2965 Amdahl's law. | For `1/((1-P)+P/S)`, P is the measured baseline fraction affected and S is its achieved speedup under a comparable workload. Include boundary/serialization/cold-start overhead. Even infinite speedup of P=0.10 bounds total speedup at about 1.111; source scarcity or downstream gating may leave publication throughput unchanged. |
| M11 | G902 3017–3039; core 330–333; YAML 120–132. | In a full 14-session window, a 70% minimum requires at least 10 operational sessions (`10/14≈71.43%`); 9/14 fails. Session count is not a time budget, and documentation sessions requested by the user must not be relabeled to game compliance. |
| M12 | Core 108–110 requires 28 consecutive complete days but mentions more than two missing telemetry days. | A missing day cannot satisfy the complete-day floor. Report observed average and observed per-day results separately; missing data is unknown even if only one or two days are absent. Use half-open local-day intervals `[00:00, next 00:00)` to include fractional timestamps without end-of-day ambiguity. |
| M13 | G902 1491; core 118 zero unsafe-job rate. | Zero tolerated unsafe publications is a policy constraint. Zero incidents observed in a finite audited sample does not prove zero true risk. Under an explicitly justified independent Bernoulli sampling model, zero events in n samples has one-sided upper bound `1 - α^(1/n)` at confidence `1-α`; independence, ascertainment and sample representativeness must be stated. Never use this statistical caveat to weaken the zero-tolerance action rule. |
| M14 | G902 2334–2381 calibration and uncertainty. | Calibration needs comparable independently labeled cohorts and mature outcomes; ECE depends on bin choices and sample size. ## Recommended artifact split

1. **Master operating prompt:** compact durable operating contract; preserve constitutions, complete publication predicates, accepted parameters and source-domain governance by reference. Add task/source separation, evidence levels, metric semantics, bounded mathematical reasoning, current task scope and honest closeout.
2. **Execution prompt:** reusable task wrapper containing objective, authority/scope, current evidence, bounded unit, files, verification and completion criteria. Do not copy numeric registries or prescribe an algorithm in advance.
3. **Maintainer bootloader:** short entry path that reads local instructions and canonical current baton, checks git/concurrency/reality, chooses the task mode, and loads only relevant deep references. It should launch authorized work rather than require rereading thousands of duplicated lines every session.
4. **This ledger:** preserves the entire conceptual coverage and rejected/conditional material so shortening the prompts does not erase rationale or turn optional research into operational orders.

Preserve unchanged by reference: core fifteen qualifying conditions, D1–D9 acceptance criteria, decision-class autonomy and graduation rules, architectural ownership, source prohibitions and compliance, canonical parameters, unit/cutover predicates, concurrency expectations and formal amendment rules. Their unresolved contradictions are recorded above for separate governed reconciliation.
