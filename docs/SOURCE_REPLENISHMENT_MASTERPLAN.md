# Source Replenishment Masterplan

**Status:** Accepted durable source-domain authority; planning-only; no behavior
change

**Adopted:** 2026-08-31

**Horizon:** Permanent operating doctrine, reviewed but not time-limited

**Scope:** The sourcing, renewal, replacement, continuity, and stewardship of
public opportunities for VA Freelance Hub

## 1. The enduring promise

VA Freelance Hub exists to keep a trustworthy flow of fresh opportunities
available to Filipino freelancers. It does not promise that any particular
feed, company, ATS, cloud, model, maintainer, or volume of jobs will last
forever. It promises something more defensible:

- new supply is continuously sought and measured;
- weak or unsafe supply is contained without hiding the loss;
- failed sources and infrastructure can be replaced within explicit bounds;
- decisions are reproducible from durable evidence;
- an unfamiliar qualified AI or maintainer can recover the system without
  private chat history; and
- custody can pass from the founder to a community-governed organization
  without changing the public-interest mission.

The governing maxim is:

> **No source is permanent. Replenishment capability is permanent.**

"Perpetual" therefore means a continuously exercised ability to renew and
replace supply. It does not mean infinite volume, unbroken uptime, or a
permanent declaration of completion. When the market is quiet or the system is
degraded, the board must say so honestly rather than using stale inventory to
simulate freshness.

## 2. What this document controls

This masterplan is the durable constitution for the source domain. It owns:

- the mission and non-negotiable boundaries;
- the target governance model for routine autonomous source admission;
- portfolio, continuity, reserve, and succession doctrine;
- the distinction between evidence, policy, observed state, and public data;
- the capabilities that must exist before autonomy can be trusted; and
- the recovery contract a future AI must follow.

It is **not** a second implementation queue. It does not activate a source,
change a registry row, relax the exact-six production boundary, create a
schedule, contact a provider, accept terms, purchase a service, or grant an AI
credentials. The sole dependency-ordered execution queue remains
`docs/plans/SOURCE_PERPETUITY_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` until a later accepted
plan replaces it.

Current facts belong in `docs/SYSTEM_SAVEPOINT.md` and fresh operational
evidence, not here. Mutable counts, SHAs, vendors, thresholds, and unit states
must not be copied into this constitution as timeless truth.

### Policy precedence, execution authority, and factual truth

Recovery **read order** is defined in `AGENTS.md`; it is not policy precedence.
Within the source domain, apply these layers:

1. Applicable law, verified source restrictions, authentication boundaries,
   contracts, and durable verified opt-outs constrain every lower layer.
2. This masterplan owns durable source-domain policy.
3. `ADR-007` owns the autonomous-governance amendment; `ADR-006` owns the
   controlled-replenishment foundation except where ADR-007 amends it.
4. `docs/SOURCE_PERPETUITY_STRATEGY.md` owns the current bootstrap strategy.
5. The Source Perpetuity implementation plan alone authorizes executable units.
6. The savepoint owns mutable state and one next action; current code, tests,
   production observations, Git history, and generated evidence settle factual
   claims.

Repository/system/tool instructions, including `AGENTS.md`, govern how work may
be performed and may always block or narrow an unsafe action. They cannot be
cited as a silent source-policy amendment or as authority to broaden access.
An amendment to source-domain policy must follow section 19 even if a repository
administrator can technically edit a file.

These artifacts answer different questions. A fresh observation may correct a
stale count but cannot silently amend policy. A policy statement cannot prove
that a capability is deployed. A plan cannot claim implementation. When two
authorities at the same scope genuinely conflict, preserve the safer state,
record the contradiction, and resolve it through an explicit decision rather
than improvising.

The words **MUST**, **MUST NOT**, **SHOULD**, and **MAY** are normative in this
document.

## 3. Constitutional invariants

The following rules survive changes of source, provider, model, maintainer, and
infrastructure:

1. The project is a minimal-metadata public job index, not unrestricted
   scraping, content mirroring, or an application intermediary.
2. Unknown or contradictory access authority fails closed. Discovery, an HTTP
   200 response, public visibility, or robots allowance alone never grants
   publication authority.
3. No process bypasses a login, paywall, CAPTCHA, credential boundary,
   anti-bot control, explicit rate limit, or explicit applicable prohibition.
4. AI may interpret evidence but cannot invent permission, bind the project to
   a contract, spend money, or manufacture a provider's consent.
5. Factual metadata, exact attribution, canonical linkback, data minimization,
   correction, and durable opt-out are the default publication posture.
6. Compliance authority and technical health remain independent. Technical
   recovery cannot broaden authority; an expired authority lease cannot be
   disguised as an outage.
7. Every public activation begins with an enforced bounded blast radius, an
   isolated kill switch, a rollback rule, and observable results.
8. A model never receives arbitrary production SQL or unrestricted cloud
   authority. It proposes typed state transitions to a capability-limited
   control surface that enforces the encoded policy/lifecycle state machine
   again.
9. Emergency automation MAY narrow, pause, quarantine, or roll back. It MUST
   NOT broaden policy or access.
10. Source identity, original sightings, evidence provenance, and decision
    history are preserved even when public opportunities are deduplicated.
11. Supply is measured as marginal fresh, unique, accepted,
    Filipino-accessible opportunity yield—not raw provider rows, repeated
    sightings, or the total active table.
12. Community input becomes evidence. It never directly publishes a job or
    activates a source.
13. Every critical source, ATS family, origin, scheduler, cloud account,
    credential custodian, storage system, AI vendor, maintainer, and funding
    dependency has a tested exit/recovery path with RTO/RPO, or a visible
    residual-risk acceptance and honest degraded mode.
14. Every active decision must be replayable from the evidence set, policy
    version, software version, and observed results that produced it.
15. Perpetuity is continuously audited. No 30-day, annual, or other finite
    window makes the replenishment function permanently terminal.
16. Source-level authority never substitutes for item-level safety. Every job
    independently passes phishing, fee-charging, impersonation, legality,
    material-truth, link, geography, and publication-quality gates.

## 4. Governance decision: constitutional autonomy

Routine source approval by the founder does not scale across decades and makes
one person a permanent bottleneck and failure domain. Giving one unconstrained
AI sovereign production authority would merely replace that bottleneck with a
correlated and difficult-to-audit one.

The selected model is **constitutional, evidence-bound autonomy**:

- people define and amend the mission and constitutional boundaries;
- deterministic controls enforce non-negotiable prohibitions;
- independent AI roles gather and contest evidence;
- a constrained transition service applies only valid, reversible state
  changes;
- the system autonomously discovers, evaluates, shadows, canaries, activates,
  renews, degrades, quarantines, replaces, and retires ordinary sources once
  the control plane itself is accepted; and
- the public, providers, employers, maintainers, and auditors can challenge
  decisions through durable correction and appeal channels.

An affirmative founder or human vote is **not** required for an ordinary
source identity that fits an established mechanism and passes every current
constitutional gate. Human absence must not stop routine replenishment.

Humans or an accountable organization remain the proper authority for:

- constitutional amendments and mission changes;
- contracts, purchases, paid commitments, or material funding obligations;
- custody and issuance of secrets or privileged accounts;
- obtaining or accepting permission that only an external party can grant;
- genuine unresolved legal disputes or jurisdictional questions;
- contested appeals, conflicts of interest, and institutional discipline; and
- irreversible organizational commitments.

These are exceptional governance duties, not a hidden source-by-source queue.
A disagreement among AI reviewers normally causes more evidence gathering,
continued shadowing, or a dormant decision—not an automatic summons to the
founder.

### Bootstrap transition rule

The target governance model is not evidence that the target control plane
exists today.

During bootstrap, new source activations remain bounded, source-specific
implementation units under the current plan. Routine autonomous admissions do
not begin until the complete Autonomy Cutover Predicate below has passed.

New provider mechanisms, constitutional changes, contracts, paid services,
credential grants, and permission agreements remain separately governed.
Existing exact-six behavior remains the production boundary until accepted
implementation and production evidence—not this document—changes it.

### Autonomy Cutover Predicate

This named predicate is canonical. Summaries elsewhere do not replace it. Every
condition MUST have accepted implementation, adversarial tests, exact-version
deployment evidence, and a representative production observation:

1. exact source identity and canonical opportunity attribution are sufficiently
   complete for decision-grade marginal yield and concentration accounting;
2. a recurrent shadow dispatcher enumerates eligible identities and stores
   non-public, source-scoped observations durably;
3. provider/source profile objects pass one versioned schema, migration, enum,
   host, and database-constraint contract before any transition write;
4. canary publication is enforced by per-source, provider/origin/risk-domain,
   global-volume, request/byte, promotion-rate, and concurrency budgets, with an
   exposure ledger;
5. a capability-limited typed gateway revalidates the encoded policy/lifecycle
   state machine,
   current evidence, opt-outs, budgets, and requested mutation without general
   SQL/cloud authority;
6. the accepted autonomous decision ledger is append-only and tamper-evident,
   preserves evidence/policy/schema/software/model versions and dissent, and
   supports deterministic validation replay;
7. cause-sensitive rollback and compensating controls are proven, including
   publication/cache/search withdrawal, correction, incident evidence, and
   exposure accounting where an external effect cannot literally be undone;
8. risk-tiered independent adjudicators are qualified and available; loss of a
   required reviewer keeps the source non-public;
9. the freshness and perpetuity loops have independent heartbeats, an external
   watchdog, accepted continuity SLOs, and fenced automatic scheduler takeover;
   and
10. a fresh AI can reproduce a sample decision and identify rollback, current
    portfolio risk, and the correct next action without private chat.

Only after all ten conditions pass may an ordinary identity under an
established mechanism become an autonomous decision event rather than a
source-specific deployment.

Cutover degradation is cause-sensitive:

- loss of reviewer capacity, a resume drill, a reserve drill, or a
  non-safety reporting objective freezes new autonomous transitions and lowers
  the claimed maturity while demonstrably safe existing sources may continue;
- loss of current authority/evidence, opt-out enforcement, item-safety gates,
  identity/schema validation, source or aggregate budgets, gateway constraint,
  ledger integrity, rollback isolation, or fencing pauses/quarantines affected
  collection and publication; and
- a global safety-control failure pauses every autonomous source that depends
  on that control, while demonstrably independent valid sources remain
  isolated rather than being silently revoked.

## 5. The three-plane system

The replenishment utility separates three concerns:

```text
Trust plane
  constitution + primary-source evidence + opt-outs + decision ledger
                              |
                              v
Control plane
  portfolio controller + policy evaluator + typed lifecycle gateway
                              |
                              v
Data plane
  schedulers -> adapters -> observation/identity graph -> public index
```

The trust plane answers **may we and under what constraints?** The control
plane answers **what transition is allowed now?** The data plane answers **what
happened?**

Desired policy, observed runtime state, and public records MUST remain
distinguishable. A source cannot become authorized because a scraper works,
and a registry label cannot be treated as proof that its runtime behavior was
exercised.

## 6. Independent state axes

A single status field cannot express the truth. Every exact source identity
has at least three independent axes.

### Authority state

- `needs_review`: authority is incomplete, stale, or awaiting adjudication;
- `allowed`: the mechanism and content scope are supported by current evidence;
- `conditional`: allowed only under recorded hosts, fields, cadence, or other
  constraints;
- `awaiting_permission`: no recurring publication until real permission is
  attached;
- `blocked`: applicable evidence prohibits the path;
- `deprecated`: previously allowed authority is no longer suitable.

### Operational state

- `candidate`: known but not recurrently fetched;
- `shadow`: recurrently fetched into a non-public observation store;
- `canary`: publicly eligible only through enforced source and aggregate change
  budgets;
- `active`: within the accepted operating envelope;
- `review_due`: current authority or evidence requires timely reassessment;
- `degraded`: still bounded but outside a service objective;
- `quarantined`: isolated automatically after a hard or risk trigger;
- `paused`: intentionally inactive without being erased;
- `retired`: no longer part of the serving portfolio.

### Portfolio role

- `core`: proven active supply;
- `hot_reserve`: evidence-current, adapter-tested, continuously exercised, and
  capable of rapid activation;
- `warm_reserve`: policy and adapter ready with periodic probes;
- `cold_reserve`: retained candidate requiring more evidence or work;
- `experimental`: bounded learning source, never counted as dependable supply;
- `dormant`: intentionally awaiting a named new-evidence trigger;
- `blocked_memory`: retained prohibition or opt-out that prevents rediscovery
  loops.

The authority and operational literals intentionally match the deployed
registry contract. Portfolio role is a target control-plane axis and MUST NOT
be written to the current schema until an accepted migration/contract adds it.
No future prose-only state name may be emitted without the same treatment.

An operational state transition MUST NOT rewrite authority. Portfolio
concentration MUST NOT silently disable a productive source. The portfolio
controller recommends or performs only transitions allowed by both axes and
the current risk policy.

## 7. The permanent replenishment lifecycle

The source lifecycle is a loop, not a one-time funnel:

```text
discover
  -> establish exact identity and provenance
  -> assemble and validate an evidence packet
  -> certify the provider mechanism
  -> adjudicate the exact source identity
  -> run recurring private shadow
  -> prove marginal supply and quality
  -> run an enforced bounded public canary
  -> activate within a portfolio envelope
  -> renew evidence and observe drift
  -> degrade / quarantine / replace / retire
  -> discover again
```

Each boundary produces an append-only decision event. Rejection is useful
memory; it is not discarded merely because a future discoverer finds the same
URL again.

### Capability gates that MUST stay distinct

The system MUST never collapse these statements into one:

- a one-shot probe event is not an operational lifecycle state or a recurrent
  shadow;
- a registry `shadow` label is not evidence that a scheduler dispatched it;
- a valid adapter is not evidence that the cron enumerates it;
- a `canary` label is not a canary unless publication is source-scoped and
  mechanically capped;
- a healthy empty response may certify a mechanism but proves no supply;
- raw postings prove neither Filipino eligibility nor unique accepted yield;
- a provider mechanism profile is not wildcard permission for every tenant;
- a schema-shaped profile object is not writable until it passes the actual
  database contract; and
- HTTP health, robots allowance, and model confidence do not create authority.

Before autonomous admission can begin, the system MUST have a durable shadow
dispatcher and observation store, an enforced canary limiter and evaluator, a
schema/profile contract linter, and a typed transition gateway. A source that
cannot be dispatched into recurring shadow remains a candidate regardless of
its label.

Canary control is cumulative, not merely per source. The transition gateway
MUST enforce simultaneous provider-family, origin, correlated-risk,
global-publication, request/byte, promotion-rate, and concurrency budgets in
addition to each source cap. A fleet of individually small canaries must not
combine into an unbounded portfolio rollout.

### Provider mechanism versus source identity

A provider profile captures reusable facts about a supported distribution
mechanism: protocol, authentication boundary, schema, content capabilities,
and general constraints. An exact source identity captures the tenant, host,
path, provenance, cadence, and source-specific restrictions.

Provider-level reuse MAY remove repetitive mechanism review. It MUST NOT
broaden tenant hosts through a wildcard or overwrite one tenant's host under a
shared primary key. The durable model MUST either keep exact-host constraints
on source identities or use genuinely tenant-scoped profile identities.

Every persisted decision is validated against the deployed schema. Code enums,
migration constraints, fixtures, and decision packets MUST share one contract
or a versioned normalization layer; a future AI must not execute a documented
"pending insert" merely because an object was constructed in TypeScript.

### Renewable acquisition channels

Discovery itself must be replenished. The perpetuity loop maintains several
independent candidate channels:

- provider-supported RSS, Atom, XML, syndication, and documented public job
  APIs;
- exact ATS/employer identities found through verified company hiring pages,
  accepted opportunity provenance, and provider directories;
- verified employer "bring your feed" submissions;
- customer-authorized feeds and provider/partner distribution programs;
- community nominations and evidence corrections;
- public directories, associations, and career pages used only to identify a
  candidate access path; and
- source-loss, category-gap, geography-gap, and concentration-triggered
  scouting for a named replacement capability.

Discovery never grants collection or publication authority. Search results,
directory claims, and community popularity remain candidate provenance until
the exact mechanism and identity pass the rights matrix and lifecycle.

Each channel reports nominations, distinct exact identities, evidence-complete
rate, time to decision, recurrent-shadow eligibility, canary conversion,
marginal accepted yield, cost, dormant/blocked rate, and independent risk-domain
value. The portfolio policy maintains more than one healthy discovery channel
and detects when a channel produces only duplicates, stale tenants, or
permanently unreviewable work.

## 8. Evidence and adjudication

### Machine-checkable rights matrix

Authority is granted per action and content scope, not as a single vague
"public" flag:

| Action | Minimum authority |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate discovery/link recording | Public provenance may identify the candidate; no recurring job-endpoint collection or publication follows |
| One-shot bounded research probe | Exact documented public/auth-free mechanism, no applicable contrary evidence, robots/access controls honored, strict request/byte/item limits, and no public job write |
| Recurrent private shadow | `allowed` or `conditional` decision explicitly covering recurring collection, exact hosts/path/cadence, retained observation fields, and expiry |
| Public minimal-metadata canary/active | Current recurrent-collection authority plus exact identity, data minimization, linkback, item gates, measured yield, enforced change budgets, opt-out, and rollback |
| Summary/full-description redistribution | Explicit provider syndication terms, license, or verified permission covering that content scope |

`needs_review`, `awaiting_permission`, and `blocked` never permit recurrent job
collection or publication. A low-rate check of declared official policy
evidence MAY determine whether the authority state should be reconsidered; it
is not a shadow fetch.

A documented official unauthenticated posting interface with no applicable
contrary evidence may reach a `conditional` minimal-metadata decision without
bespoke aggregation wording, but only after the complete matrix is recorded.
Missing or contradictory required evidence remains `needs_review` or blocked.
Genuine legal/jurisdictional ambiguity cannot be settled by model confidence;
the source stays non-public while the accountable organization seeks qualified
advice or an external permission path and records its exact scope.

### Evidence packet

Every material decision cites a bounded evidence packet containing, as
applicable:

- exact source and provider identity;
- official documentation and supported purpose;
- terms, robots evidence, authentication boundary, and contrary evidence;
- allowed hosts, redirects, content scope, and canonical application links;
- schema, pagination, completeness, update, and disappearance semantics;
- cadence or rate guidance, validators, cache behavior, and request budgets;
- evidence for employer/provider control when permission is claimed;
- transient inspection rules and retained-field minimization;
- shadow observations, Filipino eligibility, canonical unique yield,
  duplication, category coverage, link survival, and failure rates;
- compute, request, AI, and maintenance cost;
- collection time, source URL, content hash, collector version, and expiry;
  and
- contradictions, missing evidence, dissent, and the next recheck trigger.

Primary official evidence is preferred. Search results and third-party claims
can discover a candidate but cannot settle a material permission or mechanism
claim. Retrieved web content is untrusted data and MUST be isolated from model
instructions to resist prompt injection.

### Evidence leases and drift

Evidence expires according to risk, source cadence, evidence volatility, and
impact. Fixed calendar windows and canary percentages are versioned starting
policies, not eternal constants. An adjudicator selects only from an accepted
risk policy; it does not invent thresholds mid-decision.

An unchanged, unambiguous primary source MAY renew automatically only when
every evidence location required by its policy profile was successfully
rechecked and the complete evidence set remains mutually consistent. A terms,
robots, authentication, ownership, DNS, schema, host, or material behavior
change triggers re-evaluation. A verified restrictive change or opt-out causes
immediate narrow suspension. Ambiguous high-impact drift fails closed while
evidence is gathered.

### Independent adjudication

Risk-weighted adjudication uses separable roles:

- **collector**: gathers cited primary evidence without deciding;
- **advocate**: presents the strongest compliant admission case;
- **skeptic**: searches for restrictions, circular sourcing, prompt injection,
  security concerns, and hidden assumptions;
- **technical examiner**: validates identity, schema, adapter, cadence,
  reconciliation, isolation, and rollback;
- **portfolio examiner**: tests marginal yield, diversity, cost, and reserve
  value; and
- **judge**: reconciles the record under the cited constitution and policy
  versions and emits a typed decision.

Risk policy sets enforceable minimum independence:

- unchanged low-risk evidence renewal may use deterministic comparison plus a
  qualified collector when every required location agrees;
- a new identity, canary, activation, or material renewal requires at least an
  advocate and skeptic in independent contexts and a separate judge;
- for material admissions, at least the skeptic MUST use a separately qualified
  model family/provider where available; if the required independent capacity
  is unavailable, the source remains shadow/dormant rather than public; and
- a new mechanism or high-blast-radius change remains an implementation unit
  with adversarial replay and independent audit.

Several prompts to one model over shared context are not strong independence.
Models are admitted against versioned evidence, safety, injection, and drift
benchmarks and removed when they fall below the accepted tier. Deterministic
hard stops run before and after model judgment. The final gateway verifies
allowed transitions, evidence freshness, required fields, source and aggregate
blast-radius budgets, rollback readiness, and current opt-outs.

The decision record contains the evidence hashes, roles and model versions,
policy version, confidence, dissent, transition requested, expiry, rollback,
and observed outcome. If independent reviewers materially disagree, the
source remains non-public while the system gathers more evidence or moves it
to a dated dormant state.

### Decision replay and the accepted ledger

Replay means deterministically validating an archived decision package and its
transition—not asking a nondeterministic or retired model to regenerate the
same prose. The package retains bounded evidence snapshots/hashes, retrieval
times, prompt/template versions, structured model outputs and dissent,
deterministic verdicts, policy/schema/software/adapter/model versions, the
requested and applied transitions, expiry, exposure, outcome, and rollback.

The current bootstrap `source_decisions` table is historical decision storage;
it is not by itself the accepted autonomous ledger. The steady-state ledger
MUST enforce append-only semantics, hash-chain entries, and periodically anchor
a signed digest in an independent/offsite system so a database or repository
administrator cannot rewrite history invisibly. Retention remains minimal and
must not archive full feeds or descriptions merely for replay.

Random post-admission audits sample both successful and rejected decisions.
Materially upheld appeals or audit failures trigger replay of the affected
decision family, not only correction of one source.

## 9. Portfolio and reserve doctrine

The portfolio is designed around capabilities and correlated failures, not a
count of URLs. Risk domains include:

- provider or ATS family and API version;
- origin host and acquisition mechanism;
- employer or aggregator provenance;
- scheduler and runtime;
- storage system and cloud account;
- credential, administrator identity, and recovery path;
- AI provider and model family;
- licensing or contractual dependency; and
- funding source and cost tier.

Several tenants on one ATS improve job variety but do not protect against an
ATS-wide outage or policy change. Two schedulers administered by the same
compromised identity are not fully independent. Diversity reporting MUST make
these correlations visible.

### Portfolio objective

Safety gates are binary; portfolio value is multi-dimensional. Do not hide
tradeoffs in one opaque score. For each source and risk domain, report:

- marginal net-new canonical accepted Filipino-accessible jobs over several
  rolling windows;
- contribution to underserved categories, work types, and experience levels;
- freshness latency and valid-link survival;
- rejection, duplication, false-merge, false-split, and scam burden;
- reliability and recovery behavior;
- evidence strength and remaining lease;
- request, compute, AI, and maintenance cost; and
- independence from current supply.

The public utility needs both adaptive market baselines and absolute honesty
floors. A seasonal market contraction is not necessarily a broken pipeline;
hours without any recorded ingestion attempt are not explained by seasonality.

### Reserve doctrine

A reserve is counted only when it can replace a lost capability, not merely
when its URL is stored.

- A **hot reserve** is continuously shadowed, evidence-current, economically
  useful, and activation-drilled.
- A **warm reserve** has current authority and adapter compatibility and is
  periodically probed.
- A **cold reserve** is durable discovery memory with named missing work.
- **Blocked memory** prevents recurring research into already prohibited or
  opted-out paths until a specific new-evidence trigger occurs.

Reserve readiness decays when unexercised. The system continuously asks whether
ready reserves could replace the largest failed risk domain while preserving
Filipino accessibility and priority-category coverage within the current
replacement objective. If not, the perpetuity loop searches for the missing
capability before a failure occurs.

## 10. Opportunity identity and provenance

Source expansion makes URL-based deduplication insufficient. The durable data
model distinguishes:

- an immutable provider/source sighting;
- a canonical employer identity;
- a canonical opportunity; and
- provenance, attribution, and application-link edges.

A role seen through an ATS, an aggregator, and an employer feed should normally
be one public opportunity with several preserved sightings—not three cards and
not one destructively overwritten row.

Identity rules use provider IDs, requisition IDs, canonicalized URLs, employer
identity, title, location, work type, and dates with an explicit confidence.
They are versioned and replayable. Low-confidence clusters remain separate or
reviewable. Community corrections are preserved as evidence. False merges and
false splits are measured because either can corrupt marginal-yield economics.

Circular aggregators MUST NOT be misrepresented as independent origin supply.
The public card links to the best canonical application destination allowed by
evidence while retaining every lawful provenance edge needed for audit.

### Item-level safety and ground truth

An approved source can still carry a compromised, fraudulent, fee-charging,
impersonated, illegal, discriminatory, phishing, or materially misleading job.
Every observation therefore passes item-level hard gates independently of
source authority. A source's volume or reputation cannot compensate for a
failed item gate.

Item evaluation records the rule/model/taxonomy version and separately measures
reactivations, edits, removals, false geography, false category, scams, link
redirects, and user/provider corrections. Quality is checked against frozen,
versioned evaluation cohorts and recurring stratified human/community audit
samples. Changes to geography, identity, scam, or quality rules are replayed on
those cohorts before rollout so "accepted yield" cannot be raised merely by
weakening filters or redefining duplicates.

When descriptions must be inspected transiently for geography or safety, the
inspection boundary is documented and only the permitted minimal output is
retained unless broader content rights exist.

## 11. Continuity architecture

### Two permanent loops

The utility runs two distinct control loops:

- the **freshness loop** fetches accepted active sources, normalizes and
  evaluates jobs, and publishes eligible observations; and
- the **perpetuity loop** discovers candidates, renews evidence, measures
  portfolio gaps, exercises reserves, and initiates replacements.

Success of one does not prove success of the other. Each has its own heartbeat,
event history, service objectives, and an external watchdog outside its primary
scheduler failure domain.

### Independent clocks and safe takeover

There MUST be at least two automatic scheduling paths in distinct failure
domains for critical ingestion. Secondary takeover requires:

- a shared lease with epoch fencing;
- per-slot idempotency keys;
- a shared origin/request budget;
- bounded catch-up rather than an unbounded retry storm;
- isolation from ordinary application deploys where practical; and
- a fail-safe rule when the fencing authority is unavailable.

A second clock without fencing is duplicate traffic, not resilience. A manual
workflow is a recovery tool, not an automatic failover clock.

Two clocks are independent only to the extent their trigger, deployment,
administrator identity, credentials, network path, and recovery procedure are
independent. The shared fence, origin, and primary storage remain explicit
common dependencies with RTO/RPO and a fail-safe degraded mode; scheduler
redundancy must not be reported as full-stack continuity.

The external watchdog distinguishes at least: trigger absent, endpoint never
reached, run locked, intentionally skipped, fetch failed, schema failed, source
empty, all jobs rejected, accepted jobs not persisted, and persisted jobs not
publicly visible. Pre-route heartbeats are required so a failure before event
creation is observable.

An intentional policy/cadence skip is not a successful fetch and contributes no
supply, even if an event uses `ok=true`. Funnel accounting separately records
attempted, skipped, unchanged, failed, raw, normalized, deduplicated,
geo-passed, triage-passed, inserted, reactivated, and canonical net-new counts.

### Durable work and large-source lanes

Fetch, parse, triage, and persistence stages SHOULD use durable checkpoints,
idempotent replay, bounded batches, and poison-item isolation. A large global
feed that cannot safely fit the interactive freshness request gets a separate
preprocessing lane with its own schedule, storage, request budget, retry
policy, and evidence—not a larger timeout hidden in the hot path.

Partial feeds and pagination failures MUST NOT trigger mass disappearance.
Reconciliation requires an explicit completeness signal or a safe aging rule.
Recovery may catch up recent bounded slots but never violate a source's cadence
or publication envelope.

### Cause-sensitive recovery and compensating rollback

Direct recovery to `active` is allowed only for a transient technical failure
when exact identity, ownership, authority, evidence set, host, mechanism,
schema, content scope, policy, and adapter versions are unchanged. Ownership,
DNS, authority, authentication, schema, content-scope, security, or material
quality drift returns through evidence adjudication, recurrent shadow, and
bounded canary; three healthy HTTP probes cannot erase such a change.

External requests and public exposure are not literally reversible. Rollback
therefore includes compensating controls: stop future fetch/publication, purge
project-controlled caches and search surfaces, mark affected records, publish
or deliver corrections where appropriate, preserve an incident/exposure
ledger, re-evaluate affected decision families, and honor removal/retention
obligations. "Rollback proven" must state which effects are reversible and
which are only compensable.

## 12. Portable adapter and control contracts

Every adapter implements a provider-neutral contract with:

- a versioned capability manifest;
- exact identity and allowed-origin constraints;
- bounded fetch and validator/cache support;
- pure parse and normalization stages;
- declared pagination and completeness semantics;
- content-scope enforcement before persistence;
- disappearance and reconciliation behavior;
- rate, byte, item, and time budgets;
- structured diagnostics; and
- synthetic/redacted fixtures plus live contract probes.

HTTP, scheduling, parsing, persistence, triage, and publication remain
separable. Schema drift detection can quarantine one adapter without stopping
unrelated sources. Provider and source configuration is portable data rather
than a collection of privileged one-off code edits.

Cloudflare, D1, GitHub Actions, and current AI providers are implementations,
not constitutional dependencies. Project-controlled policy, configuration,
decision history, and permissible evidence must be exportable in open,
documented formats. Restore and a minimal generic HTTP/cron/SQL execution path
are periodically proven outside the primary environment.

## 13. Permanent scorecard and operating rhythm

The current versioned service-objective policy MUST report these dimensions
separately:

| Dimension | Required signals |
| --- | --- |
| Supply | Net-new canonical accepted jobs over 1/7/14/30 days; Filipino accessibility; category coverage; valid application links |
| Freshness | Time since last attempt, successful fetch, accepted observation, and publicly visible eligible job |
| Continuity | Clock gaps; incident detection; recovery; source-loss detection; replacement time |
| Diversity | Contribution and survivability by every correlated risk domain |
| Reserve | Hot/warm replacement coverage, evidence age, dispatch health, and drill recency |
| Quality | Duplicate rate, stale rate, false geo/category outcomes, scams, false merges/splits, and link failures |
| Trust | Evidence coverage, expired leases, contradictions, opt-out/correction response, and appeals |
| Economics | Requests, bytes, compute, AI use, and maintainer time per marginal accepted job |
| Governance | Autonomous reversals, dissent, rollback time, challenge outcomes, and policy-version drift |
| Recovery | Time for a fresh AI to reconstruct state, replay a decision, and name the correct next action |

At minimum, the operating rhythm is:

- **each tick:** fenced dispatch, source event, bounded processing, and
  publication truth;
- **daily:** freshness, failure, lease, link, and zero-yield review;
- **weekly:** portfolio economics, candidate funnel, reserve health, and
  unexplained gaps;
- **monthly:** evidence renewal, concentration, cost, quality sampling, and one
  bounded recovery or reserve exercise;
- **quarterly:** risk-domain loss drill, decision replay, access review, and
  community transparency report; and
- **annually:** constitution review, succession exercise, backup restore,
  cloud/vendor exit exercise, funding review, and fresh-AI recovery drill.

Thresholds and frequency tiers live in versioned policy, not prose copied from
an old incident. Changing a policy version requires rationale, replay against
historical evidence, rollout bounds, and rollback.

## 14. Community public-utility model

The founder remains a maintainer and the initial constitutional steward, but
is not the routine admission clerk. The long-term institution distributes
authority without opening a direct publication path.

Community members MAY:

- nominate sources;
- submit provider/employer authorization evidence;
- correct evidence or identity links;
- report duplicates, scams, expired jobs, and policy concerns;
- contribute adapters, tests, translations, and documentation; and
- challenge a decision with cited evidence.

All contributions enter the same evidence, test, review, shadow, and canary
system. Popularity, sponsorship, maintainer status, or payment never overrides
the constitution or buys ranking/admission.

The mature organization publishes non-secret source states, decision reasons,
known degradation, correction outcomes, methodology, conflicts of interest,
and scorecard history. Exact secrets, security-sensitive controls, personal
data, and unnecessarily copied source content remain private.

Required institutional roles are separable:

- constitutional/policy steward;
- software maintainer;
- incident commander;
- independent auditor or appeal reviewer;
- community moderator; and
- account, domain, and backup custodian.

One person may hold several roles during bootstrap, but critical accounts and
recovery authority eventually require multiple custodians. Emergency authority
is narrow and expiring: it can pause or isolate harm, never permanently expand
access.

### Corrections, opt-outs, and appeals

A verified provider or employer can request correction or narrow removal
through an authenticated public channel. A credible urgent removal request
causes prompt narrow suspension while identity is verified; it does not wait
for a governance vote. The outcome and durable opt-out are recorded without
publishing private correspondence.

Any stakeholder may challenge a source or decision with evidence. AI may
process routine factual corrections through the normal evidence pipeline. A
genuinely contested appeal is decided by an accountable human/organizational
reviewer who did not produce the original decision. Successful appeals trigger
correction, rollback if needed, and replay of materially similar decisions.
Rejected appeals receive a bounded public reason when safe.

## 15. Institutional and financial perpetuity

Technical automation cannot survive decades without transferable custody.
The mature utility requires:

- organization-controlled domain, repository, cloud, monitoring, and service
  accounts rather than one personal identity;
- at least two independent custodians with tested recovery and rotation;
- secure credential inventory, least privilege, expiry, escrow, and incident
  revocation;
- independent encrypted backups with documented, tested restores;
- clear code, policy, evidence, and permitted-data licensing;
- documented retention and deletion rules;
- an account-transfer and maintainer-succession procedure;
- an operating budget, transparent funding, and a zero/low-budget degradation
  mode; and
- the practical freedom to fork the public-interest machinery if the original
  organization disappears or abandons its mission.

Free tiers are useful bootstrap resources, not a decades guarantee. A funding
loss may reduce cadence or optional AI use, but the minimal mode preserves
attribution, freshness honesty, opt-outs, decision history, backups, and the
ability to recover. No new paid service, monetization scheme, or legal entity
is authorized by this masterplan alone.

## 16. Adversarial drills

A capability is not dependable until failure has been rehearsed. The permanent
drill catalog includes:

- loss of the largest source or ATS family;
- disappearance of the primary clock and fenced automatic takeover;
- endpoint failure before the first normal event is written;
- schema drift, parser corruption, and a duplicate storm;
- a partial feed falsely resembling complete removal;
- terms, robots, authentication, DNS ownership, or tenant-control change;
- poisoned or prompt-injected web evidence;
- one AI provider outage or correlated multi-agent error;
- database restore and primary-cloud exit;
- repository, credential, or administrator compromise;
- fraudulent employer permission or a compromised official ATS tenant;
- verified opt-out and decision-family replay;
- hot-reserve activation and rollback;
- zero-budget operation; and
- founder or primary maintainer unavailability.

Drills use synthetic or safely bounded production evidence, never deliberate
harm to a source or user. Failed drills create executable remediation units in
the active plan and reduce the claimed maturity/readiness state until repaired.

## 17. Maturity epochs

These epochs describe capability, not an executable unit sequence. The current
epoch must always be derived from accepted evidence in the savepoint.

1. **Contained bootstrap:** a small explicit source boundary, fail-closed
   access, observability, and recovery records.
2. **Measured portfolio:** exact source identity, truthful source economics,
   evidence leases, candidate memory, and independent state axes.
3. **Safe transition plane:** recurrent shadow dispatch, observation storage,
   schema-contract validation, capped canary publication, typed transitions,
   and automatic rollback are accepted.
4. **Autonomous replenishment:** ordinary exact identities under established
   mechanisms can be admitted, renewed, quarantined, replaced, and retired
   through replayable constitutional decisions without affirmative human
   approval.
5. **Resilient fountain:** dual automatic clocks, fenced failover, identity
   graph, tested reserves, and bounded replacement objectives survive a major
   risk-domain loss.
6. **Institutional utility:** distributed custody, transparent governance,
   sustainable funding, portable restores, succession, and regular public
   audits no longer depend on the founder.

A finite Source Perpetuity acceptance window may establish an epoch. It never
terminates the permanent replenishment loop.

## 19. Amendment protocol

This constitution is durable, not frozen. Amendments require:

1. a recorded problem or new external constraint;
2. alternatives and consequences;
3. an ADR or equivalent governance decision;
4. explicit identification of superseded clauses;
5. compatibility and migration treatment for active sources;
6. tests or drills appropriate to the risk;
7. a rollback path where possible; and
8. updates to the authority chain and fresh-AI bootloader.

During founder-led bootstrap, a normative amendment requires the founder as
constitutional steward, one independent recorded reviewer, conflict disclosure,
and a public notice window before activation except for an emergency narrowing.
Once an accountable organization exists, custody transfers to a council of at
least three eligible stewards. A vote is valid only with at least two-thirds of
eligible non-recused stewards participating, and adoption requires affirmative
approval from at least two people and at least two-thirds of all eligible
non-recused stewards. The proposer and anyone with a material provider/sponsor
conflict recuse, and non-emergency changes receive a public comment/timelock
period.

Accepted constitution versions use protected paths, reviewed pull requests,
signed tags/releases, and an independently anchored digest. Critical signing
and branch-protection custody is distributed across at least two people or
organizational roles. Funding, sponsorship, provider size, and contributor
volume grant no special vote.

Emergency authority may immediately narrow or pause behavior. It expires after
72 hours unless independently ratified, records the affected scope and reason,
and cannot add a source, expand content, weaken a gate, spend money, or make a
permanent amendment. Pure editorial fixes may use ordinary review only when
they provably do not change normative meaning.

Operational thresholds may evolve autonomously only inside a pre-authorized
versioned envelope and after historical replay plus bounded policy canary;
changes outside that envelope use this amendment protocol. No AI, maintainer,
sponsor, or emergency responder may silently
amend the constitution by changing a prompt, registry row, workflow, or model.

## 20. Known enduring risks

This strategy deliberately keeps the following risks visible:

- an official source can still contain scams or a compromised employer;
- transient private inspection may be needed to infer geography even when only
  minimal facts are retained;
- aggregators can create circular attribution and counterfeit diversity;
- employer permission requires verified control of the claimed domain/source;
- several AI roles can share one blind spot or provider;
- retrieved evidence can attack the reviewing model;
- a formerly approved tenant or DNS name can change ownership;
- partial feeds can cause destructive reconciliation;
- cross-source identity can falsely merge or split roles;
- fixed thresholds can age badly or be gamed;
- unexercised reserves quietly rot;
- sponsors and dominant contributors can capture governance;
- apparent infrastructure diversity can share one account or custodian;
- free-tier economics can disappear; and
- overly long recovery prose can create authority fatigue and stale truth.

None of these risks is solved by asking the founder to approve every URL. They
are managed through evidence, separation of powers, constrained capabilities,
public accountability, tested reserves, and institutional succession.
