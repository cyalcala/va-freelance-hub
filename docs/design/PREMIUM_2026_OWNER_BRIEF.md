- Linear-style precision
- Raycast-style utility
- product density
- responsiveness
- immediate interaction feedback

The preferred blend is:

**Premium editorial warmth × Linear precision × Raycast utility × modern fintech trust**

This combination should make the redesign feel genuinely new rather than merely prettier.
