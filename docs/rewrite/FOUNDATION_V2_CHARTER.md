## Purpose and priorities
A useful, honest, sustainable index of permitted remote opportunities for Filipinos. In strict order: (1) no unauthorized charges or services, (2) source rights and truthful verified publication, (3) availability and recovery, (4) useful relevant coverage and freshness, (5) simplicity and measured efficiency, (6) extensibility. Optimize under constraints; do not optimize a vanity metric at the expense of an invariant. The target is USD 0 incremental infrastructure cost, not a guarantee from a pricing page. If zero cost and a proposed service level conflict, reduce work or decline the feature; never silently upgrade. Existing subscriptions, device electricity, domain renewal and owner time are separate explicit costs, not free compute.

## Architecture and boundaries
One portable Rust CLI/batch runner with pure policy core and adapters (HTTP, source ATS/RSS/API, SQLite-compatible lake, AI, publishing). Start as one deployable binary, not microservices. Preserve Astro HTML/UI and routes, SEO/canonical URLs, attribution, source policy and existing fixtures. Turso may remain durable acquisition lake if free-tier usage is proven. D1 becomes optional bounded publication/control mart, never required for a public request. Compile immutable content-addressed static artifacts outside the visitor path; publish verified generations and atomic pointer/deployment; serve ordinary pages directly from Cloudflare static CDN. Client-side search with partitioned index and bounded payload; pre-render crawlable listing/detail pages where feasible. Never force Worker-first routing. Source collection, AI, analytics, full scans and rebuilds never run on visitor requests. No automatic fallback to expensive compute. Use local SQLite for bounded batch staging if useful; no unbounded storage or PII/raw-description retention.

Last-known-good is durable and rollback-tested, not merely isolate memory. Enforce expiry and removals even on degraded paths: explicitly distinguish stale snapshot from currently valid jobs; hide expired/withdrawn records and legally required takedowns via tested bounded removal/republication procedure, do not claim stale jobs fresh. Preserve verified original posting date and exact source/authority. If urgent removal cannot be guaranteed, suspend affected public surface. Verify artifact size, counts, schema, per-record gate, manifest hash, monotonic version, public probe, rollback. Never publish an empty/partial generation solely because a run succeeded; legitimate empty sets require explicit verified evidence.

## Rust implementation targets
Start with contracts, budget, time, identity, scheduling, normalization, qualification, publication and snapshot pure logic, then acquisition/refinery/AI adapters. Proposed logical modules hub-core, hub-budget, hub-scheduler, hub-fetch, hub-parse, hub-identity, hub-geo, hub-refinery, hub-intel, hub-publish, hub-snapshot, hub-verify and CLI. Group modules where simpler; avoid needless crates. Tokio/reqwest/serde/rusqlite or compatible libSQL adapter, SHA-256 or BLAKE3 with explicit versions; benchmark before choosing extra dependencies. Bounded streaming, checked arithmetic, cancellation/deadlines, per-host pacing, conditional fetch, retry-after, no raw SQL bypass. Keep TypeScript as reference behavior; fix known defects deliberately rather than copying them. Shared golden fixtures compare Rust output with intended policy; discrepancies need adjudication, not blind parity.

## Stop conditions and success
All ordinary static paths incur zero D1 reads and no Worker script execution (measured). No uncontrolled clocks, retries, paid service, or hidden work; the runner may defer, pause or sleep. Each published record has current source and job evidence, independent public exposure and truthful receipts. Maintain verified last-known-good through provider failure. Demonstrate backup restoration and rollback independent of provider deployment history. Establish observed p50/p95/p99 latency, verified unique first publication flow, recall/precision and quota utilization with UNKNOWN where not measured. At least 30 days of ordinary runtime and billing observation including a reset boundary and delayed charges; never simulate a paid quota incident by creating spend. No guarantee of permanent free service or 100/day supply. Stop adding machinery once product usefulness and safe operation are demonstrated; any future change reopens only affected gates.
