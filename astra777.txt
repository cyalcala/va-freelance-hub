## Architecture

row, bundled last-known-good). Intake is apps/web/src/pages/api/cron/scrape.ts.

before assessPostingFreshness. Employer confidence is not an input to either.

## Trusted employer strategy

Employer PH confidence, job validity, PH eligibility, and role relevance stay
separate. Confidence is evidence that the employer hires Filipinos. It does
not publish a closed, stale, duplicate, onsite, or country-locked opening.
Direct job country and telecommuting beat the employer label. There is no
global allowlist. Pineapple Staffing is the counterexample: three open US
telecommuting roles from 2020–2022, public count 0, and that zero is correct.
