before dedup. Conflicting title, shortcode, remote flag, or original date
holds the URL with `IDENTITY_CONFLICT`, `REMOTE_UNKNOWN`, `REMOTE_CONFLICT`,
or `POSTING_DATE_CONFLICT`. A date-only `published_on` stays that UTC calendar
day. `created_at` is not a posting date. PH text in the joined location is
the geo decision. An explicit non-PH lock such as "US citizens only" still
rejects. Generic "citizens of" does not. Structured PH/APAC/worldwide scope

is not wired by the deployed scrape route.
